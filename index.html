Motion and UI exploration

Educational use only. This is a prototype built to study motion and interface design. It is not a real store: nothing is sold, no payments are processed, and no data is sent over the network. Prices are simulated.

Some people experience discomfort, dizziness or nausea from motion on screen. If that happens, turn on Reduce motion in your operating system settings. These animations will then be disabled.

Things to try

  1. Switch between Flat and Neumorphism in the header to compare design styles.
  2. Use the sun and moon toggle to switch between light and dark themes.
  3. Hover over a watch card to see the lift and photo zoom.
  4. Add a watch to your cart to see the check mark and burst feedback.
  5. Open the sort dropdown to see the spring-based menu and staggered rows.
  6. Move across the style filter to see the morphing tooltip.
  7. Hover near a primary button to see it lean toward the pointer.
  8. Go through checkout to see the step transitions, the loading to success button and the count-up total.
  9. Remove an item from the cart to see the remaining rows slide up.
Prototype simulator. No real payments, no network requests. Prices in USD.
